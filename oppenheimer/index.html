Everything goes back to 1947.

Oppenheimer cuts between his life in colour, a 1954 hearing in colour, and a 1959 hearing in black and white. Put on one calendar, the dated story runs 1930 to 1963, 33 years in 180 minutes. Two of its scenes reach back to the same conversation: Strauss accuses from 1959, 12 years later, and the last flashback answers from 1963, 16 years later.

The three strands, by the calendar

  1. His life, in colour1930 to 1963, 33 yearsOppenheimer's own story, from study in Europe to Los Alamos and after

Why colour and black and white

Nolan described the colour scenes as subjective and the black-and-white scenes as objective. source

He wrote the screenplay in the first person, his first time doing so. source

The closing flashback moves the weight of the film from Strauss's wounded pride to Oppenheimer's guilt. source
