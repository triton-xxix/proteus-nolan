TENET

A bullet both ways.

Tenet runs forward for an hour and then runs back through itself. This page lets you scroll it in both directions. the 14th is the only day it names.

Start at the opera or read it in the order it happened.

2 The entry

1Kyiv opera housethe 14th

The siege at the opera

A CIA team goes into a staged terrorist siege to pull out an asset. The Protagonist is compromised. A masked man with an orange trinket on his bag saves him, and a bullet in the seat beside him un-fires. His team is caught and tortured.

It is the first day of the film and, as it turns out, the last. The masked man is Neil. The bullet is the first inverted object we see.

Forward: the Protagonist

2Unknownday unstated

The pill

Under torture he takes the suicide pill. It is a fake. He wakes to be told he was the only one who passed.

Tenet recruits by seeing who would die for the mission. He did.

Forward: the Protagonist

The word, and the bullet that goes backwards

He is given one word, Tenet, and a hand gesture. A scientist, Barbara, shows him bullets whose entropy runs backwards: drop one and it jumps back to his hand. Something in the future is sending them.

The rules of the film are set here, in a single room, and nothing later breaks them.

Forward: the Protagonist

Mumbai: Neil and Priya

He traces the inverted rounds to an arms dealer, Priya Singh, with a new partner, Neil. Priya says the rounds come through a Russian oligarch, Andrei Sator, who can talk to the future.

The Protagonist has just met Neil. Neil has known him for years.

Sir Michael Crosby points him at Sator's estranged wife, Kat, an art appraiser. She once authenticated a Goya forged by a man called Arepo. Sator holds the forgery over her and keeps their son from her.

The forgery sits in a freeport in Oslo. Stealing it is the way in.

They crash a cargo plane into the freeport to open its vault. Inside is a machine with two doors, and two masked men come out of it in opposite directions. The Protagonist fights one. Neil chases the other and lets him go.

Both men are the Protagonist, a week from now. He is fighting himself and does not know it.

The film says out loud what it has just shown. From here the audience knows more than the hero.

The deal that puts them all on the Tallinn highway.

The highway heist, and a car driving backwards

They take the case from an armoured convoy at speed. A car joins the chase travelling backwards. An inverted Sator, with Kat as hostage, demands the case. The Protagonist hides it and the case that reaches Sator is empty.

The backwards car is the Protagonist too, from later tonight. The case holds the last piece of something, not plutonium.

An inverted bullet in a forward body is fatal unless the body is inverted too.

The chase, from the other side

The heist is shown twice, once from each direction, and both are the same event.

SATORAREPOTENETOPERAROTAS

A Latin word square that reads the same forwards, backwards, up and down. The film's five proper nouns are its five words, and TENET sits across the middle both ways.

  • SATOR Andrei Sator, the oligarch
  • AREPO the forger of the Goya
  • TENET the organisation, and the word
  • OPERA the Kyiv opera house where it opens
  • ROTAS the security firm at the Oslo freeport

The rules the film plays by

  • Inversion is done with a turnstile. You walk in moving forward and walk out moving backward, and you meet yourself on the way.
  • An inverted bullet un-fires: it leaves the wall and returns to the gun. Barbara shows the Protagonist one in the briefing.
  • Inverted people cannot breathe ordinary air. They carry their own.
  • Fire runs the wrong way for an inverted body. An explosion freezes you rather than burning you.
  • The film's own line for free will inside a fixed sequence is "What's happened, happened". Neil adds that it is faith in the mechanics of the world, not an excuse to do nothing.
  • The grandfather paradox is raised and left open: the future may be gambling that it does not apply.

Days the film does not name

One day is named: the 14th. Two durations are spoken: kat needs days inverted for the wound to heal, and the oslo freeport turnstile is about a week before tallinn. Everything else is an order, not a date.
