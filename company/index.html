When two chairs changed at once

  • Memento (2000), after Following: new cinematographer (Wally Pfister) and new editor (Dody Dorn).
  • Batman Begins (2005), after Insomnia: new editor (Lee Smith) and new composer (Hans Zimmer and James Newton Howard).
  • Tenet (2020), after Dunkirk: new editor (Jennifer Lame) and new composer (Ludwig Göransson).
