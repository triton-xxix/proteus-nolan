Why cut it this way

Per Wikipedia, Nolan built the film around the characters' points of view, wanted images rather than dialogue and backstory to carry it, and wanted a snowballing effect of separate storylines connecting, something he had only used before in his third acts. A paraphrase, not a direct quote. source
