Time

5 of 7 films

Structure as subject: the order you learn things in is the story.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 1998 Following

    The film opens in a clean, controlled scene and then jumps between different points in the young man's story. The viewer assembles the order from clues, such as his changed appearance, rather than being told.

    source

  2. 2000 Memento

    The opening plays a Polaroid un-developing, a gunshot undone and a body restored. From there the story moves away from its own ending, putting the audience in Leonard's position of arriving mid-scene with no idea how it got there.

    source

  3. 2010 Inception

    During the climax the film cuts among several dream levels at once, each running at its own speed, while a van falls in slow motion on the topmost one. The deadlines tie the clocks together.

    source

  4. 2014 Interstellar

    After a brief landing on a water world near the black hole, the crew return to their ship to find that 23 years have passed for the man left aboard. The short trip has quietly cost them more than two decades.

    source

  5. 2017 Dunkirk

    The hour in the air, the day at sea and the week on the beach are cut together as if they happen at once, with a ticking score pushing all three forward. The audience feels the clock without being told which one it is watching.

    source

Deception

5 of 7 films

Tricks played on characters and on the audience, usually at once.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 1998 Following

    The man he trusts has set him up from the start. After the break-in at the Bald Guy's safe, the burglar uses the young man's own tool and fingerprints to leave him looking guilty, so the interview he gives is built on a lie the audience also believed.

    reading, no source

  2. 2000 Memento

    Near the end Teddy tells Leonard what has really happened. Leonard chooses not to keep that knowledge and writes himself a note that sends his future self after Teddy. He deceives himself on purpose, so the hunt can go on.

    source

  3. 2002 Insomnia

    Dormer plants evidence and bends the account of the shooting to protect himself, while a young local officer starts to see that the official story does not fit the facts. The film asks whether a good cause excuses a lie.

    reading, no source

  4. 2006 The Prestige

    The film closes on the revelation that 'Borden' was two identical men sharing one life. Looking back, the secret was in front of us the whole time while the story pointed our eyes at the hands, the diaries and the stage.

    source

  5. 2010 Inception

    The final shot cuts away as a spinning top seems to wobble, leaving the viewer unsure whether the scene is real. The film plays tricks on its characters and its audience in the same move.

    reading, no source

Guilt

3 of 7 films

A past act the film circles back to.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 2000 Memento

    The film slowly suggests Leonard played a part in his wife's death. Choosing to forget that and keep hunting someone else is his way of avoiding it.

    source

  2. 2002 Insomnia

    Dormer shoots his partner in fog during a pursuit, and then lets the death pass as the suspect's work. From there the guilt follows him into a hotel room where light leaks round the blinds and he cannot lie still.

    reading, no source

  3. 2010 Inception

    Cobb's dead wife appears inside the dreams he builds and keeps wrecking the jobs. She reads as a projection of what he cannot forgive himself for, which is why she intrudes exactly when the team needs a clean run.

    reading, no source

Sacrifice

3 of 7 films

Someone pays so that others can go on.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 2006 The Prestige

    Each trick demands something real in return. Angier's solution to the Transported Man leaves him drowning in a tank beneath his own stage while the audience applauds above, and the film asks what a person owes to art that they cannot get back.

    source

  2. 2014 Interstellar

    Cooper detaches his module and falls toward the black hole so that the rest of the crew has enough momentum to continue the mission.

    reading, no source

  3. 2017 Dunkirk

    A civilian skipper takes his small boat across the Channel to pick up strangers, knowing the crossing is dangerous. His small act is one of many that together change the outcome.

    source

Obsession

2 of 7 films

A pursuit that costs more than it can return.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 1998 Following

    The young man, bored and lonely, picks strangers in the street and trails them for the sake of it, first with a set of loose rules and then ignoring them. The habit that begins as a writer's research grows until it takes over his days.

    source

  2. 2006 The Prestige

    Angier, determined to beat his rival's teleportation trick, takes a machine from Tesla and stakes his own life on it. Night after night he steps into the apparatus and lets the show continue, whatever it does to him. The wish to win has overtaken every other reason.

    source

Memory

2 of 7 films

What a character cannot remember, or cannot stop remembering.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 2000 Memento

    Leonard cannot hold onto anything new, so he relies on Polaroids, scribbled notes and tattoos as his memory. The film keeps showing that these records are only as trustworthy as whoever made them, and that he is the one who made them.

    source

  2. 2010 Inception

    Nolan has said the heist framing was too light for a story about dream and memory, so Cobb was given an emotional wound to carry. The dreams are built from remembered places and people, which is how the past leaks into the job.

    source

Grief

2 of 7 films

Loss as the engine of a plot.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 2000 Memento

    Leonard's purpose is built on the death of his wife. Because he cannot move forward, his mourning has no end, and his revenge can be restarted again and again without the hunt ever concluding.

    source

  2. 2010 Inception

    Cobb cannot go home, and the whole job is a way to buy back his old life and see his children again. A loss drives every decision he makes in the film.

    reading, no source

Duality

2 of 7 films

Doubles, mirrors, rivals, two halves of one thing.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 2002 Insomnia

    Dormer and the killer Finch talk by phone and then meet, and each recognises that both have done something they cannot take back. Finch uses this to pull the detective into a shared secret, so the hunter and the hunted start to resemble each other.

    reading, no source

  2. 2006 The Prestige

    The two magicians are built as mirrors: one a hands-on working-class craftsman, the other a polished showman. Neither is allowed to be simply the hero or the villain, and the twin secret makes the doubling literal.

    source

Duty

2 of 7 films

Doing the job in front of you, whatever it costs.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 2002 Insomnia

    Burr, the junior officer, has to choose between the rules she has been taught and the loyalty she feels towards a detective she admires. Her choice sets the moral shape of the ending.

    reading, no source

  2. 2017 Dunkirk

    A pilot keeps flying on with a failing fuel gauge, rationing what he has left so he can keep protecting the ships below. His job is the only thing he is thinking about, and he treats the cost as arithmetic.

    reading, no source

The machine

2 of 7 films

A device, a method or a weapon that outgrows its maker.

98000205060810121417202326

  1. 2006 The Prestige

    Angier travels to meet Nikola Tesla, who builds him a device that duplicates whatever stands in it. The invention works better than anyone wants, and the film sets it beside the real Tesla and Edison rivalry as a second war running behind the first.

    source

  2. 2014 Interstellar

    TARS, a blocky robot built from sliding slabs rather than a human shape, works alongside the crew with an adjustable honesty setting. Nolan has described the robot as a friendly counterpart to a famous earlier film computer.

    source
