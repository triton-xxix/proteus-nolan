Fixed points

  • Presented order stated by Wikipedia: opening credits reversed, 1, V, 2, U, 3, T, 4, S, ..., 22/A, credits. Opening colour sequence V is the shooting, played in reverse. source
  • Convergence is 22/A: black-and-white 22 becomes colour A while a Polaroid develops. No timecode given. source
  • Chronological order (DVD hidden feature on 2-Disc Region 1 and 3-Disc Region 2 editions): credits reversed, 1-21, 22/A, B-V, opening title forward. Story begins at mono 1, ends at colour V, the shooting shown first on screen. source
  • Salon letters page confirms the label 22/A and ties it to Leonard copying the licence plate. source
